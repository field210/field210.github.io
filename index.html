yt-automation

yt-automation is a private tool used only by its owner, to automate tasks in his own Google account.

What it does

It runs Google Apps Script automations that read, label, and organize Gmail messages and update related Google Sheets and Drive files. It is also used with personal productivity tools such as Tasker and Raycast.

How it uses Google data

The app accesses Google user data only to perform these tasks for the owner's own account. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
